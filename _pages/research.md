My research focuses on higher geometric groupoids and the morphisms between them, with particular emphasis on higher Lie theory and differential geometry. Groupoids provide a natural framework for describing geometric structures with symmetries, while their higher-categorical generalizations, higher groupoids, encode richer geometric and homotopical information. Guided by the Yoneda philosophy, I am interested in understanding these objects through the morphisms between them and the notions of equivalence they induce. In geometry, strict morphisms often fail to capture the appropriate notion of equivalence between geometric structures, motivating the study of generalized morphisms represented by principal bundles and bibundles. These lead naturally to Morita equivalences and correspondences, which provide a categorical framework for comparing geometric objects. My research explores these ideas in the higher-categorical setting, particularly through higher Morita equivalences and higher correspondences of geometric infinity-groupoids.
